Aisha Altenhofen

Aisha Altenhofen (1997, North-Rhine Westphalia) is living between Berlin and Zurich. Her artistic practice deals with dislocation and photography, as well as the instability of text and image.

Aisha Altenhofen is an artist. She graduated from the University of the Arts in Berlin in 2025. Recent exhibitions include: Graduation Show, University of the Arts, Berlin (2025), AABB, Dorothea Konwiarz Stiftung, Berlin (2024), First Exposure, Austin Space, Munich (2024), trouble!, Cittipunkt, Berlin (2023), 1,2, Treize, Paris (2023) and Profiling, Museum of Photography, Berlin (2022).
